Privacy policy

What TikWave stores, why, and how you stay in control.

What TikWave stores

  • Your account: email address, username, password (never in plain text: only a fingerprint of it is kept), profile photo, banner, bio and status.
  • Your library: your sounds, playlists, likes, posts and appearance settings, so you can find them on all your devices.
  • Your online sounds: audio files prepared for online listening are stored with a file host (Cloudflare R2).
  • Your Premium subscription: if you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe. TikWave never sees or keeps your card number; it only keeps a Stripe customer ID and your subscription end date.
  • Two functional cookies: one keeps you signed in, one remembers the language you picked.

Where the data lives

The site runs on Render, the database is hosted by Neon (PostgreSQL), and online audio files by Cloudflare. Confirmation emails are sent through an email delivery service.

Advertising

Free accounts may see an ad banner provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or identifiers to show ads and measure how well they work. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads are only shown with your consent, which you can change at any time from Google's consent message.

Learn more: how Google uses data from sites that use its services · manage the ads Google shows you.

With Premium, this banner is neither loaded nor shown.

TikWave does not sell your data.

Your rights

You can view and edit your information from your profile. You can also delete your account from your profile: your playlists, posts, likes and synced library are then erased, and any subscription is cancelled. To access your data, correct it or object to its processing, write to the site's publisher.
